# Prioritization, scope, estimates, and collaboration

Use this module when a design choice must become a feasible plan with accountable ownership.

## Greedy loading rule

For a focused request, load every packet in this module unless its player moment is demonstrably outside the request. For a complete repository diagnostic, load every listed packet. The packets contain the operational procedure, evidence standard, repair patterns, deliverable rubric, and failure checks.

## Packets

- [game-design-feature-prioritization](./capabilities/15-game-design-feature-prioritization.md): rank work by player value, evidence, cost, risk, and reversibility.
- [game-design-ideal-outcome-backcasting](./capabilities/25-game-design-ideal-outcome-backcasting.md): work backward from a concrete ideal player story.
- [game-design-smart-goal-audit](./capabilities/49-game-design-smart-goal-audit.md): make design goals observable and decision useful.
- [game-dev-budget-estimator](./capabilities/56-game-dev-budget-estimator.md): estimate cost as ranges driven by scope and delivery assumptions.
- [game-dev-first-steps](./capabilities/57-game-dev-first-steps.md): sequence early work toward a playable decision proof.
- [game-dev-team-gap](./capabilities/58-game-dev-team-gap.md): identify missing capability, capacity, and ownership before commitments.
- [game-dev-time-estimator](./capabilities/59-game-dev-time-estimator.md): estimate schedule ranges with dependencies and rework.
- [software-team-conflict-patterns](./capabilities/61-software-team-conflict-patterns.md): retain the decision-quality method relevant to design collaboration.

## Cross-domain checks

When a finding changes the player path, also load the adjacent module for fairness, pacing, player context, social impact, economy, and delivery feasibility. Do not treat this index as a substitute for the packets.
