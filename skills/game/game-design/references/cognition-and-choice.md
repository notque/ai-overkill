# Cognition, perception, and player decisions

Use this module to audit information, timing, perceived causality, and voluntary choices.

## Greedy loading rule

For a focused request, load every packet in this module unless its player moment is demonstrably outside the request. For a complete repository diagnostic, load every listed packet. The packets contain the operational procedure, evidence standard, repair patterns, deliverable rubric, and failure checks.

## Packets

- [game-design-attribution-audit](./capabilities/03-game-design-attribution-audit.md): audit what players believe caused success and failure.
- [game-design-fogg-behavior-audit](./capabilities/18-game-design-fogg-behavior-audit.md): test whether ability, opportunity, and prompt meet at a voluntary action.
- [game-design-thinking-fast-and-slow-audit](./capabilities/52-game-design-thinking-fast-and-slow-audit.md): match decision time, cue clarity, and reversibility to stakes.

## Cross-domain checks

When a finding changes the player path, also load the adjacent module for fairness, pacing, player context, social impact, economy, and delivery feasibility. Do not treat this index as a substitute for the packets.
